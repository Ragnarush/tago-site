What TAGO does

One app instead of the spreadsheet, the shoebox of receipts and the notebook in the glove box.

Invoicing

  • PDF quotes and invoices with your logo, numbered, GST and QST calculated.
  • An accepted quote becomes an invoice in one step.
  • Deposits and partial payments, each with its own PDF receipt.
  • Reminders for unpaid invoices, credit notes to correct an issued invoice, statements of account.
  • Income without an invoice (platforms, cash) counted too.
  • Not registered for taxes yet? Invoices without GST or QST, and a notice near the small-supplier threshold.

Receipts and expenses

  • Photograph a receipt, or share several (photos, PDFs) to TAGO.
  • Merchant, date and amounts read on the device: the photo is never sent anywhere.
  • Likely duplicates flagged before saving; returns subtracted from your expenses.
  • Every expense carries its T2125 and TP-80 line, and its business-use share.
  • Suppliers and purchase orders as PDFs.

Hours by zone and mileage

  • Draw your work sites on a map and clock in with a tap.
  • Optional automatic arrival and departure detection, off by default.
  • A client's hours over a period added to an invoice in one step.
  • A mileage log per vehicle, odometer on January 1 and December 31 with photo, free and unlimited.
  • With Pro: a trip measured by GPS. Only the total is kept, never the route.

For the accountant

  • Excel workbook for the year or a quarter.
  • The period's receipt photos and PDFs in one ZIP.
  • Tax and deduction estimates (income tax, QPP, QPIP).
  • The lines of your GST/QST return.
  • Home-support tax credit statements and help with RL-24 slips.
  • The year archived in one ZIP readable without TAGO.

Backup

  • All your data in one file encrypted with a passphrase.
  • On demand or automatically.
  • Restore on a new phone, and check a backup without overwriting anything.
  • Optionally, an encrypted copy in your Google Drive, unreadable by Google and by TAGO.

Calendar

  • Optionally, your tax deadlines in your phone's calendar: dates, never amounts.
  • Your time-tracking sessions too, if you want.

Your data stays with you

TAGO has no server and you have no account to create. Your invoices, receipts and hours live on your phone, and what you export (PDFs, Excel, backups) stays yours, with or without TAGO.

What TAGO doesn't do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics added by TAGO.
  • Nothing is sold or shared.
  • Receipts are read on the device.

The five network exchanges

There are five, all listed in the policy: the zone map (OpenFreeMap); error reports without an identifier (Sentry); technical metrics two Google components send on their own, without the content of your receipts; and, if you turn them on, email through Brevo and the encrypted backup on your Google Drive.

Quebec's Act 25

A privacy policy built into the app, a designated person responsible for personal information, and explicit consent before the first clock-in and before automatic detection.
