TAGO guide

The essential steps, in the order you'll need them. In this guide, “⋮” is the menu at the top of the screen, and ⋮ › Backup means: open the menu, then tap “Backup”.

1. Getting started

On first launch, TAGO asks Where do you work?: your province sets the taxes on your invoices and the estimate of your deductions. Then, on the Home screen, the Getting started card lists the five steps that make TAGO useful: enter your business and your province, add a first client, create a first document, scan a first receipt (or, if you track your hours, create a time-tracking zone), and set up a backup. Each step opens the right screen and checks itself off once done.

Put your name, logo and tax numbers on your invoices

⋮ › Company profile. What you enter here appears on every quote and invoice: name, contact details, address, province or territory (taxes), tax numbers (GST/HST and, depending on the province, QST, PST or RST), NEQ and RBQ licence in Quebec, and logo.

If you change provinces or register for taxes along the way, TAGO asks since when: the profile's Tax history card keeps your previous profile, and each invoice, income or receipt is counted with the profile in force on its date.

Your payment instructions (Interac address, who cheques are payable to, direct deposit), the default due date, numbering and the PDF's look are set in ⋮ › Invoices and PDF.

Not registered for taxes yet? Leave the numbers empty: your invoices will go out without tax, and TAGO will warn you as you approach the small-supplier threshold.

2. Where to find things

Four tabs at the bottom of the screen: Home, Revenue, Expenses and Time tracking. Everything else is in the ⋮ menu, in three groups:

GroupEntries

In lists, the magnifying glass at the top of the screen searches for a supplier, a client, a number or an amount. Press and hold a row to select it, then drag to select several: the top bar then offers to select all, share (for a spreadsheet or a calendar) or delete, with Undo to take it back.

3. Invoices and payments

From a quote to the invoice

In Revenue, tap New document and create a quote. When the client accepts it, open it and tap Mark accepted: you can then create its invoice without retyping anything.

Send an invoice

Open the document: on a draft, the main button, Send by email, goes through your phone's email app. PDF preview shows what the client will receive; if it isn't next to it, it's under More actions (the ⋮ at the bottom left). To send straight from TAGO, connect your own Brevo account (free) in ⋮ › Invoices and PDF; a help screen explains how to get the key, and Send directly (Brevo) is then added to the actions.

Record a payment, even a partial one

Open the invoice and tap Record a payment. Each payment keeps its date, its method and its own PDF receipt; the balance due updates.

Remind a late client

An invoice past its due date becomes “Overdue”. Open it and tap Send a reminder: the reminder goes out with the balance due.

Correct an invoice already sent

An issued invoice can no longer be edited: that's the rule that keeps your records reliable. Open it, tap More actions, then Issue a credit note. Taxes are adjusted for you.

Make the same invoice every month

Open last month's invoice, More actions › Duplicate, then adjust the date and the lines.

Income without an invoice (platform, cash)

In Revenue, open Income without an invoice and tap Add income. It counts in the accountant's file and toward the small-supplier threshold, but stays out of the invoiced total.

Send a statement of account

⋮ › Clients, open the client's record and tap Statement of account: it shows what the client owes you to date. The Statements of account button on the client list sends them to several clients at once.

Stop retyping your services

Save them once in ⋮ › Products and services, then, when filling in an invoice, tap From catalog.

4. Receipts and expenses

Scan a receipt

On Home or in Expenses, tap Scan a receipt. TAGO reads the merchant, date and amounts on the device; check them before saving. If TAGO suspects a duplicate, it tells you first.

Add an invoice you received by email (PDF)

In your email or files app, use Share and pick TAGO. You can share several photos or PDFs at once.

You lost the receipt

In Expenses, under “No receipt?”, tap Add without receipt and enter the expense by hand.

An expense that's partly personal

For a cell phone or vehicle you also use personally, open the expense and fill in Business use (%), under “For your accountant”. The expense amount stays whole; the estimates of your income and of your tax credits count only that share, and the accountant's workbook shows the percentage.

A return or refund is entered with This is a return or a refund: it's subtracted from your expenses.

5. Time tracking by zone

Create a work zone

In Time tracking, switch to the Zones view and tap Add a zone: place it on the map and adjust its radius. You can then clock in with a tap in the Tracking view (Clock in at…), with no location permission: clocking in by hand reads no position.

Clock in automatically on arrival

⋮ › Automatic time tracking. It's off by default; TAGO asks for your consent, then Android asks for location permission. Your location is processed on the phone and never sent.

Automatic tracking didn't trigger

The usual causes: location turned off, a battery saver putting TAGO to sleep, or a zone that's too small. Check that detection is turned on in ⋮ › Automatic time tracking, and that Android lets TAGO use your location all the time. A missed session can be added by hand with Add a session, in Tracking.

Tracking keeps the last five sessions; See full history opens Time history, filterable by zone and period, with the time tracked. Tap a session to correct it, or press and hold to select several and share them as CSV or .ics, or delete them.

Bill your tracked hours

In Tracking, under “Recent history”, tap Bill on a finished session: its hours are added to a new document or to a draft you've already started, and you set the rate on the next screen. The session then shows Billed and can't be billed twice.

To bill all of a client's hours over a period at once, start from the invoice instead: choose the client, then tap Import hours for a period in the editor and tick the sessions to add.

6. Mileage

In Time tracking, the Trips view keeps a log per vehicle: business trips, and the odometer reading on January 1 and December 31 with a photo. It's free and unlimited.

Under Possible trips, TAGO suggests trips from your tracked zones. Nothing is added on its own: each trip waits for your confirmation. The trip list filters by month and by vehicle; press and hold to select trips and share them as CSV. The year's log goes to your accountant as an .xlsx from ⋮ › For my accountant.

Measure a trip by GPS (Pro)

Tap Measure a trip (GPS). TAGO can also offer it when the phone detects you're driving (Offer a trip when I drive, in ⋮ › Automatic time tracking); without your tap, nothing is measured. Only the total kilometres are kept, never the route.

7. For the accountant

⋮ › For my accountant prepares what you're asked for at the end of the year or quarter:

  • an Excel workbook (summary in formulas, revenue, expenses);
  • the period's receipt photos and PDFs in one ZIP;
  • the year's mileage log (.xlsx);
  • the year's archive, readable without TAGO.

On Home, once your tax numbers and filing frequency are entered, the Your GST/QST to date card (GST/HST outside Quebec) shows the net tax for the current period and its due date. Deductions calculation estimates the income tax and contributions to set aside, in every province (QPP and QPIP in Quebec, CPP elsewhere). Your situation, your deductions and the year's rates can be checked and edited in ⋮ › Taxes and deductions.

TAGO estimates; it doesn't replace your accountant. Have your returns checked.

8. Backup and new phone

Back up your data

⋮ › Backup. First choose a passphrase and write it down elsewhere: it encrypts the file. As soon as it's saved, TAGO writes an encrypted backup every day to the phone's Documents/TAGO folder (Android 10 and later), plus a light archive, without photos, every hour. A backup that stays on the phone is lost with it: under Local backup, use Change folder (or Choose a folder) for a folder synced off the phone, or turn on Cloud backup, which puts an encrypted copy in your own Google Drive. Back up now makes one on the spot, and Automatic backup picks where the regular backups go.

Check a backup

Check a file (or Check an online backup) confirms a backup is intact and would restore, without overwriting anything on the phone. TAGO also does it on its own once a month for your latest backup, and a card on Home warns you if it's damaged.

Switch phones

  1. On the old phone, make an up-to-date backup.
  2. Install TAGO on the new one. On first launch, Restore a backup; otherwise, ⋮ › Backup › Restore from a file (or See my online backups if it's on Google Drive). Enter your passphrase.
  3. Then redo what doesn't travel in the file: your Pro purchase (Restore my purchases), your Brevo key, and where your backups go (check ⋮ › Backup).

You forgot the passphrase

No one can open a backup without its passphrase, not even us: that's what protects it. If the app is still installed, choose a new passphrase and make a new backup right away.

9. TAGO Pro

The free tier keeps up to 10 client records, 25 documents and 25 receipts. TAGO Pro, a one-time $34.99 purchase in ⋮ › TAGO Pro, removes these limits and adds GPS trip measuring, plus a trip offered when the phone detects you're driving. On a new phone, Restore my purchases brings it back with the same Google account.

10. Frequently asked questions

Does TAGO work without the Internet?

Yes. Everything happens on the phone. Only the zone map, direct email sending, the Google Drive copy and buying TAGO Pro need the network.

Do I need to create an account?

No. TAGO has no account and no server. Your data stays on your phone and in the files you export.

When do I need to register for taxes?

As a general rule, when your taxable revenue goes over $30,000 over four consecutive quarters (the small-supplier threshold). TAGO warns you as you approach it. Check your situation with the Canada Revenue Agency (Revenu Québec in Quebec) or an accountant.

Does TAGO work outside Quebec?

Yes, since version 1.34: TAGO calculates GST, HST, QST, PST and RST based on your province and your client's (the place of supply), and prepares the accountant's file and deadlines for your province. Since version 1.35, the deductions estimator covers every province and territory too: federal and provincial income tax, QPP in Quebec or CPP elsewhere, and QPIP in Quebec.

Is there an iPhone version?

It's planned, but not available yet.

What does Quebec's Act 25 ask of me, with my clients' contact details?

A person in charge of protecting personal information (you, by default), rules published in plain terms, an incident register, and an answer within 30 days to a client who asks for their information. The details, and what TAGO does for you: Quebec's Act 25 and you.
